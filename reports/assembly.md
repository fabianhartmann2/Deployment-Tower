# Assembly and service sequence

This is a **de-energized mechanical prototype sequence**, not an electrical work instruction. Keep the C8 cord disconnected throughout mechanical assembly and service. Only a competent/qualified person may define and perform mains assembly, inspection, energized tests, or work behind the power-compartment cover.

## Preconditions

1. Print and pass the applicable coupons in [print_plan.md](print_plan.md).
2. Confirm the freshly regenerated [validation_results.md](validation_results.md) has no `FAIL`; review every WARN and PENDING item.
3. Measure the actual Mac, APV, antennas, C8, extensions, inserts, plugs, grommets, liner, and cables. Update parameters/retention geometry and regenerate outputs where results differ.
4. Confirm the intended two-port allocation before wiring: one RUTM30 port serves the internal Mac link and the other serves the single rear-panel Ethernet extension.
5. Have a competent electrical designer define the complete single-inlet topology, protection, materials, separation, shrouding, restraint, labels, inspection, and test plan.
6. Select screw heads/lengths and inserts from coupon and joint-stack results. Verify tool access, engagement, torque, pull-out, perpendicularity, and no boss cracking/breakthrough.

## Mechanical assembly order

1. **Inspect prints.** Deburr only non-calibrated edges. Reject warped interfaces, cracked flexures/bosses, incomplete walls, or damaged cable/connector edges.
2. **Install verified inserts.** Fit the M3 and M4 inserts in their paired shell/compartment bosses using the insert maker's process. The vertical `coupon_m4_seam_insert` trial selected the one-notch Ø5.4 mm pilot. Include the two router-retainer towers, four handle-foot inserts, four upper power-cover bosses, two blind APV pilots, two C8-island inserts in the full-depth Ø4.2 pockets, six seam inserts, and two cap-lock inserts only after the selected insert is proven compatible with its available depth. The logo panels no longer use threaded inserts; install their magnets separately with controlled polarity and suitable adhesive.
3. **Mount the power box mechanically.** Seat revised `power_compartment`, shifted 3 mm forward on the +X side, on the two lower-shell rails and fasten its four unchanged floor positions from inside the completely empty box with provisional low-head M3x8 screws. The corrected model keeps each diameter-3.4 bore, diameter-6.2 head area, and diameter-4.5 driver corridor open; verify all four screws can be started and tightened before fitting the APV or cover. Confirm the raised C8 centre at `(X,Z)=(38,116)` and its fixed island/tunnel align with the rear-panel clearance island, both raised tie bridges are clear, and the nominal diameter-12 Mac-AC gland remains unobstructed.
4. **Dry-fit the unenergized power hardware.** With the compartment already shell-mounted and its cover removed, lower the actual APV onto the two supplier-coordinate axes and install its fixing screws from above into the blind insert/captive concept. Verify the diameter-3.6 lug clearance, washer/head access, insert retention, and no floor breakthrough. Use the raised bridges for a restraint mock-up without drilling the floor. The four wall-tied upper bosses accept a provisional M3x8 cover stack with 5.2 mm nominal engagement. The stepped 6 mm Mac-AC solid is only a reserved routing envelope: branch hardware, conduit/barrier construction, lead bends, topology, and electrical acceptance remain PENDING. Do not wire or energize from this sequence.
5. **Join the body modules.** Before the router tray and cap are installed, place six provisional low-head M4x18 screws into the upper shell's internal lugs. The physically measured Ø7.0 heads move laterally through 8.4 mm internal entry slots and must sit freely within the Ø8.2 x 3.2 pockets. Lower the upper shell onto the three locating keys and the lower shell's six inserts across the 0.8 mm shadow seam. Start and tighten the screws evenly with a long hex driver through the six internal top corridors. The three axes per side are at Y -50/10/50; no screw or opening is exposed on the outside. Confirm all six start freely, the internal U-belts meet without collision, and there is no step, rocking, bottoming, forced fit, or tool contact with other parts.
6. **Install the revised router tray.** Retire the physically loose tray with the broken rear flexure. Fit four tested 8 x 16 x 1.3 mm compliant pads to the printed lands. Fasten the four top-loaded positions into the two upper-shell cross rails using M3x6 screws with measured heads no larger than Ø6.2 x 3.0; the revised Ø6.2 x 2.2 recesses leave about 0.7 mm nominal clearance below the supported router. Install two M3 inserts in the new rear towers. Verify the inward-shifted guides, lowered top ledges, screw heads, lands, and +Y withdrawal corridor are clear.
7. **Install the revised Mac cradle.** Retire the original loose/right-handed print. Fit three approved 2 mm compliant pads into the 1 mm seats of the rear-left/Ø112/0.30-clearance revision; the intended nominal pad top is the Mac support plane, but compression remains a physical test result. Fasten the four cradle ears from below into the lower-shell bosses with provisional M3x10 hardware. Keep the annular intake and button corridor completely clear.
8. **Load and retain the Mac from below.** With the base removed, use the lead-in cams to deflect the four positive-Z clips as the Mac rises horizontally into the cradle. Confirm all four overhangs have their nominal non-contact top gap, the two linked bottom release rails remain reachable, and no printed feature or provisional wear pad marks the Mac, intake, button, finish, or port/plug keep-outs. Fit, force, preload, scratch, shake, and cycle acceptance remain PENDING.
9. **Dry-fit the button and intake.** Temporarily attach the base with the four provisional M3x14 screws. Operate the native button through the continuous lower-rear path and inspect the diameter-116 opening against the physical intake/feet. Remove the base for cable dress.
10. **Install low-voltage/data hardware.** Mount the accepted single Ethernet flange, top-down HDMI PCB, and both USB-C PCBs in the V7-derived rear-panel geometry. Route the Mac Ethernet, HDMI, both USB-C leads, router DC, and the single Ethernet-extension lead in the low-voltage lane with measured service loops and accessible releases. Keep them outside the relocated +X power compartment, mains keep-out, Mac air/button/removal paths, and all fastener tips.
11. **Load and screw-retain the RUTM30.** Leave both rear corner stops off. Present the router at the open rear (+Y) end with its RF row facing rear, then slide it toward the front (-Y) until the fixed front stop is reached. Place `router_rear_retainer_left` and `router_rear_retainer_right` over their towers, move each slotted stop forward until it just meets the outer chassis corner without loading an SMA connector, and secure it from above with one M3x8 screw and washer. Confirm all four pads share support, the router does not rock or scrape, the approximately 0.3 mm physically predicted side clearance remains free, and the native Ethernet face has clear internal lead routing.
12. **Populate the one-piece rear panel.** Dry-fit the selected single RJ45 extension directly in its two-hole M3 flange pattern. Install four cellular SMA and two Wi-Fi RP-SMA bulkheads directly in the six Ø6.6 rear-panel holes, using the 2.0 mm clamping lands and the sequence Mobile / Wi-Fi / Mobile / Mobile / Wi-Fi / Mobile. Torque only to the connector supplier's limit, route the six short coax leads without sharp bends, and add independent strain relief so antenna loads do not reach the router ports. There is no separate router-interface bezel.
13. **Close the rear service face.** Offer the panel over the fixed C8 island without moving the inlet or inner cover. Install the eight provisional M3x8 rear-panel screws. Confirm rear withdrawal needs neither cap/handle nor antenna removal.
14. **Install the magnetic logo panels.** Bond two 6 x 3 mm magnets into each shell receiver and two 6 x 1.5 mm magnets into each panel pocket. Mark and verify polarity before adhesive cure. Seat each panel directly in its existing recess; confirm a flush, non-rattling fit and serviceable pull-off force. No logo screw or finger scallop is used.
15. **Build and attach the cap/handle subassembly.** With the cap detached, install four M3x14 screws upward from its underside into the four inserts in the handle's 8 mm feet. Verify 5.0 mm nominal engagement and full bearing on both cap pads. Slide the complete cap/handle subassembly laterally onto both upper-shell dovetail rails using the physically selected 0.10 mm-per-side fit. From inside the still-open enclosure, install the two concealed M4x10 anti-slide locks. The dovetails carry vertical load; the locks prevent lateral escape. No screw is visible on the top face.
16. **Close the base.** Dress cables clear, then install its four provisional M3x14 screws and verify all feet are stable. The base, cradle, recessed router-tray, and general service stacks deliberately use different lengths.
17. **Proof-check before carrying.** Confirm the cap is fully seated on both dovetails, both hidden M4 locks are installed, all four underside handle screws remain tight, and neither cap nor feet show gaps or cracks. Do not carry equipment until insert pull-out, joint torque, layer adhesion, creep, and controlled proof-load tests pass.

## Intended handle load path

`continuous Ø22 rounded grip -> spherical shoulders -> two Ø24 round legs -> Ø32 flared roots -> two 42 x 32 x 8 mm radiused feet -> four underside M3x14 screws and blind foot inserts -> cap bearing pads/crossmember -> two transverse dovetail rails -> upper-shell crossbars and U-belt -> six hidden M4 shell-seam joints -> lower-shell U-belt, walls, rear-sill angle beam, and equipment supports`

Computational validation checks four clear screw axes, surrounding foot-insert material, broad foot/pad bearing witnesses, both selected dovetails, two hidden M4 cap locks, zero installed interference, and the M3x14/M4x10 length stacks. With the provisional 1.8 kg complete mass, the 4x design proof target is 70.6 N total, or 17.7 N per screw under ideal equal sharing. This is a test target, not an allowable load or capacity claim. It does not perform FEA or establish insert pull-out, material strength, layer adhesion, safe working load, fatigue, impact, creep, or unequal load sharing. Replace the provisional mass with the measured complete mass and proof-test a safely restrained prototype before carrying.

## Routine service sequence

1. Shut down equipment as required; disconnect the external C8 cord, data cables, and antenna connections needed for access; follow the approved electrical procedure; verify de-energization.
2. For cable/interface service, remove the eight rear-panel screws and withdraw the panel along +Y. It moves around the fixed inlet island; leave the separate power cover closed.
3. Replace an extension, patch lead, liner, or low-voltage support without opening the mains compartment. Recheck releases, bends, service loops, and pinch points.
4. For router service, disconnect its RF, Ethernet, and DC connections, remove the two M3x8 rear-stop screws and both corner stops, then withdraw the router along +Y through the rear opening. The four recessed M3x6 tray screws then become accessible if the tray itself must be removed. Reinstallation proceeds from +Y toward -Y; never flex the rigid corner stops around the router.
5. For Mac service, remove the four M3x14 base screws and lower the base. Disconnect the internal Mac leads, use the two bottom-access release rails to deflect the four Z-retention clips outward, and lower the Mac. Release/remove the four cradle screws only if the cradle also needs service. Validate force, hand access, and the fully wired downward-removal sequence on hardware.
6. The Wi-Fi antennas unscrew from their rear RP-SMA bulkheads; the bulkheads remain in the one-piece rear panel unless coax service is required. Pull each magnetic logo panel straight out of its recess with a non-marring suction tab or protected edge tool; neither operation should expose the power cover.
7. Open the power-compartment cover only as a separate deliberate operation by an authorized person under the reviewed electrical procedure.
8. Reassemble in reverse order and inspect every cable, flexure, insert, and joint for wear or damage.

## Physical post-assembly checks

- Stable four-foot support and retained measured air gap.
- Twenty intentional Mac-button operations from desk and handled positions, with no accidental actuation.
- Unobstructed Mac intake/exhaust and instrumented temperature comparison under representative load.
- Fully wired Mac downward removal using both release rails and router removal along +Y without cable/structure interference.
- Four Mac clip/cam engagements, wear pads, two linked release rails, three 2 mm base pads in 1 mm seats, and at least 20 insert/release/shake cycles without damage or unintended release.
- Four router compliant pads share load without rocking, excessive compression, vent obstruction, or screw-head contact; the two rigid adjustable stops retain the rear corners without touching SMA hardware; repeated screw/removal and +Y service cycles remain smooth.
- Full seating, latch/release access, retention, and functional test for HDMI, both USB-C extensions, and the single Ethernet extension.
- Concurrent six-bulkhead RF connection/disconnection with correct nut torque, no rotation, edge abrasion, connector load, or tight coax bend.
- Rear-mounted Wi-Fi antennas withstand handling/shake and at least 20 screw cycles without loosening bulkheads or loading router connectors.
- Logo panels remain flush, non-rattling, and removable after at least 20 cycles.
- Insert/screw joints survive defined torque and repeated service without spin-out or cracked bosses.
- The handle joint survives torque/service cycles, insert pull-out screening, and a safely restrained 4x measured-assembly-weight proof load plus creep dwell before carrying.
- Qualified electrical inspection and applicable tests pass before any routine energized use.
