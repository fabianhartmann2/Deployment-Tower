| Evidence | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| `tests/test_parameters.py` | Envelope/ratio, rejection of unsafe partial scaling, distinct joint fits, immutable independent configurations |
| `tests/test_geometry.py` | Required parts/coupons, validity/build, connected installed solids, official router/connectors, collision sensitivity, power ties/APV/cover/gland/route, Mac clip/cam/release, router pad/recess, only-handle allowlist, genuine handle mating, and aggregate zero-FAIL geometry gate |

| `python -m deployment_station.export` | Every registered STEP reopened, every STL checked, geometry/report/manifest/renders regenerated, failure-gated exit |
| `python -m deployment_station.validation` | Current-source geometry plus existing STL report |

Use the freshly regenerated [validation_results.md](validation_results.md), `validation.json`, and `export_manifest.json` for exact test/validation/artifact counts and hashes. This traceability table intentionally does not preserve pre-regeneration totals. Computational results do not close any physical, thermal, load, signal, cable-routing, or electrical acceptance row.
